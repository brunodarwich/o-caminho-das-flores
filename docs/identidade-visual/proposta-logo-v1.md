# Proposta de logo — O Caminho das Flores

Estudo visual gerado em 27/09/2026 com a ferramenta nativa image_gen. Proposta para avaliação estética; não substitui a logo canônica nem foi aplicada ao portal.

Referências lidas: weboon-info.txt, docs/NARRATIVE_STORYTELLING.md e docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM_STITCH.md. Referências visuais: logo do Capítulo 1 e banner-ocdf-c1.png.

Conceito: preservar o lettering orgânico e a identidade botânica, dando maior protagonismo ao título. O caminho curvo sugere a jornada de autoconhecimento de Ariel. A flor lavanda é uma interpretação simbólica do florescimento, não uma representação canônica validada da Flor do Luar. Flores laranja e folhas verdes preservam a ligação cromática com a marca atual.

Arquivo: proposta-logo-v1.png. Texto conferido visualmente. O resultado apresenta um halo de fundo; recorte/transparência de produção e legibilidade em tamanhos pequenos ainda precisam de refinamento após a escolha da direção.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE beautiful original proposed title logo for the Brazilian fantasy webtoon. Exact and only text: "O Caminho das Flores". Use image 1 as reference for the existing logo's botanical identity, hand-inked flowers, colors and warmth; do NOT reproduce its dense circular floral frame or plaque. Image 2 is reference for the author's gentle hand-drawn comic atmosphere and organic shapes, not a character to include.
Narrative: Ariel, a green elf botanist, studies a rare moon flower, struggles with perfectionism, loss and anxious thoughts, and learns presence, compassion and letting go. Blue sky and passing clouds symbolize inner calm. Make the design feel like an intimate magical illustrated story, with quiet wonder and emotional warmth.
Design a distinctive expertly crafted hand-lettered wordmark, airy yet confident, readable as a small webtoon title. Three-tier cohesive composition: small "O" above, large "Caminho", small connecting "das", and prominent large "Flores" below. Elegant warm organic storybook lettering, gently flared strokes, open counters, flowing but clearly readable, not generic wedding calligraphy, not blackletter. Dark forest-green lettering with a restrained warm ivory edge to hold together over different backgrounds. Integrate a graceful winding ochre footpath into the lower swash of the wordmark, rising toward a single small lavender-blue flower with five rounded petals and a pale center, a symbolic botanical motif rather than a claim to a canonical species. A few sage-green leaves and two modest apricot orange blossoms asymmetrically balance the lower corners. Preserve large breathing spaces between plants and letters. Title is 80% of visual importance. Restrained lavender, sage, forest green and apricot colors drawn from the reference. Delicate hand-ink contours and minimal flat cel shading matching a polished independent comic. Clean crisp silhouette, masterful typography, vector-friendly simplicity. No enclosing wreath, no solid plaque, no scene, no characters, no mockup, no extra labels, no credit line, no slogan, no photorealism, no 3D, no glow. Generous clear margins, centered landscape lockup. Truly transparent background.
```
